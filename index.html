How to survive

01
Explore

Tap a nearby tile, swipe the board, use the rune pad, or tilt your device. Fog lifts as you move.

02
Fight

Step into a foe to strike. Every action gives the dungeon a turn. Use potions and your class skill wisely.

03
Descend

Claim the Ember Sigil on each floor to unseal the stairwell. Reach floor five and defeat the Ember Warden.

04
Grow

Gold, chests, and fallen foes make you stronger. Leveling restores health. There is no second life.
